Bio

Yakuza 0 is an action adventure game where you play as Yakuza members Kazuma Kiryu and Goro Majima as they try and uncover a plot involving an empty lot. each character has 3 unique fighting styles to help handle any situation. explore the 2 detailed locations Kamurocho and Sotenbori with tons of exploration with a huge variety of minigames from Karaoke to undeground fighting colosseums
