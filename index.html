Bio

Yakuza 0 is an action adventure prequel to Yakuza 1 released on March 12th 2015 in Japan and January 24th 2017 in North America and Europe By the game studio Ryu Ga Gotoku (Like a Dragon) where you play as Yakuza members Kazuma Kiryu and Goro Majima as they try and uncover a plot involving an empty lot. each character has 4 unique fighting styles to help handle any situation while you explore the 2 detailed locations Kamurocho and Sotenbori with tons of exploration with a huge variety of minigames from Karaoke to underground fighting colosseums
